● About

A small tool that does exactly one thing

KEYCHECK is a free online keyboard and mouse tester. It shows you whether your input devices are delivering their events correctly — and nothing else. No account, no installation, no telemetry, and no interest in what you type.

Advertisement

What the tool does

  • Keyboard test — renders a full 104-key ANSI reference layout, highlights every physical key you press, counts the total presses, counts how many different keys you have tried, and shows the coverage as a percentage. A running history of the last presses is displayed in order.
  • Mouse test — counts left, right, middle and side button clicks, registers scroll wheel events and the distance they report, follows the pointer path inside the test area and gives an indicative double-click measurement with the interval in milliseconds.
  • Honest limits — the page tells you what a browser cannot check, so you do not mistake a clean result for a hardware diagnosis.

How it is built

The site is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no framework, no analytics package and no external font or script dependency. Keys are identified with the physical code property of the keyboard event rather than the character it produces, which means the detection works the same way on QWERTY, AZERTY and QWERTZ layouts. Every counter is updated by local JavaScript; the site has no backend that receives keystrokes, because there is no backend for that purpose at all.

What we deliberately do not do

  • We do not record, store or transmit the keys you press.
  • We do not use keyloggers, session recorders or behaviour tracking.
  • We do not ask for permissions we do not need — no camera, no microphone, no clipboard, no notifications.
  • We do not require an account, and we do not sell or share personal data.
  • We do not show artificial or forced advertising, and the ads never overlap the interactive tool.

How the site is funded

Hosting a site like this costs money, so the pages may carry advertising served by Google AdSense. That is the only monetisation: there are no affiliate links, no product catalogues and no paid tier. The tester itself remains free and fully functional whether or not ads are shown, and the advertising layer is technically independent of the tool — if the ad script fails to load, nothing else is affected.

Accuracy and honesty

We describe what the tool measures in precise terms, because a tester that overstates itself is worse than no tester. The page never claims to diagnose hardware failures, never invents usage statistics and never publishes claims about results that have not been verified. When a limitation exists, it is written down on the page itself rather than hidden.

Get in touch

Found a bug, a key that behaves differently on your setup, or a question the FAQ did not answer? The contact page is the fastest way to reach us, and corrections are always welcome.
